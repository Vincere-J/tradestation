VINCERE TRADINGALGO STRATEGY REFERENCE
Squeeze ORB Trend
All times are EST (New York time).

SQUEEZE

Identifies tight consolidation periods where price is coiling. Enters on the directional breakout when energy releases.

ORB (OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT)

An opening window sets the high/low range. Trade fires when price breaks out above or below the range.

TREND

Catches sustained upside moves when momentum turns bullish. Long only.

SQUEEZE ALGOS
B2XSqueeze
Micro Russell (M2K)Long & Short
Squeeze after a strong push. Takes profit at three levels so the last contracts can run.
ENTRY
Waits for a strong move one way, then for the other side to try to take control and fail. Enters with the original push on a candle close once its proprietary confluences confirm.
EXITS
3 profit targets, fixed stop, trailing stop on the remaining contracts.
SIZING
Same size every trade. Edge Leverage off.
URGOSqueeze
Micro Nasdaq (MNQ)Long & Short
Squeeze on Micro NASDAQ. Bigger moves, smaller size than B2X.
ENTRY
Waits for price to stall in a tight range without picking a direction, then enters on a candle close once its proprietary confluences confirm a direction. The close does not need to break the range.
EXITS
3 profit targets, fixed stop, trailing stop.
SIZING
Same size every trade. Edge Leverage off.
G4MSqueeze
Micro S&P 500 (MES)Long & Short
Squeeze on Micro S&P 500. Does not use an opening range.
ENTRY
Waits for price to coil, then enters on a candle close that its proprietary confluences confirm as the move out of the squeeze.
EXITS
3 profit targets, fixed stop, trailing stop on what is left.
SIZING
Same size every trade. Edge Leverage off.
OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT ALGOS
OGXObadex GladiatorORB
Micro Nasdaq (MNQ)Long & Short
Core ORB on Micro NASDAQ. Range 9:35 to 9:55 AM. Most actively traded algo in the suite.
ENTRY
Enters the moment price moves through either side of the range. It does not wait for a candle close.
EXITS
Profit targets, fixed stop, trailing stop.
SIZING
Edge Leverage on: the next trade after a stop out that day uses more contracts.
RBOORB
Micro Russell (M2K)Long & Short
ORB on Micro Russell 2000. Range 9:10 to 10:00 AM. Small-cap, tends to diverge from NQ/ES in choppy markets.
ENTRY
Trades a break of the range, long or short.
EXITS
3 profit targets, fixed stop. The stop moves into profit after a gain, then a trailing stop takes over.
SIZING
Same size every trade. Edge Leverage off.
TREND ALGO
MST2Trend
Dow (YM)Long only
Trend on the Dow (MotusTemplar), full size YM at every account size. Buys sustained moves when momentum turns bullish.
ENTRY
Waits for a dip or a pause in an uptrend to run out of steam, then buys at the close of the candle where its proprietary confluences confirm the uptrend is resuming.
EXITS
3 profit targets, fixed stop, trailing stop.
SIZING
Same size every trade. Edge Leverage off.

CHOP WARNING

ORB algos perform best on clean directional opens. False breakouts in choppy sessions are the algo following its rules, not a malfunction.
