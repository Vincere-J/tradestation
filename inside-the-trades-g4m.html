How G4M trades

Looks forA squeeze style algo on the 15 minute Micro S&P 500 (MES) chart. It does not use an opening range. It waits for a 15 minute candle to close with its proprietary confluences lined up, then enters long or short.
What it waits forPrice coiling on the 15 minute MES chart, then a candle close that its proprietary confluences confirm as the move out of the squeeze.
Entry triggerOnly on a 15 minute candle close. All 8 entries in this backtest filled exactly on a close (9:45 AM, 10:15 AM, 10:45 AM twice, 11:30 AM, 11:45 AM, 12:45 PM, 1:15 PM). The window opens at 9:35 AM, so the first close it can act on is 9:45 AM. It does not use an opening range or wait for price to touch a level.
Managing the tradePT1 takes 2 contracts off at 20 pts (the same distance as the stop), PT2 takes 1 at 30 pts and PT3 takes the last at 40 pts. Once the trade is 20.25 pts in profit, the stop on what is left trails 10.25 pts behind the best price, moving in 1.25 pt steps. Breakeven was off in these backtests, so the trail did the protecting.
When it trades9:35 AM to 1:30 PM ET, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. No new longs from 10:30 to 11:00 AM and no new shorts from 11:00 to 11:30 AM.
Stop and targetsStop 20 points from entry, 3 targets, 20, 30 and 40 points away. A trailing stop starts after 20.25 points of profit and follows 10.25 points behind. Any trade still open closes at 4:30 PM EST.
Contracts and whyAlways 4 contracts, split 2, 1 and 1 across 3 targets. Planned risk about $400.
In the backtestBacktest period 10/1/2026 to 10/23/2026: 8 trades, 5 winners and 3 losers, net +$965.90 after fees.
Patterns in this backtest8 trades on 8 trading days, never more than 1 trade a day. 4 long and 4 short. Entries from 9:45 AM to 1:15 PM ET. Of the 8 trades: 5 reached PT1; 2 reached PT2; 5 had contracts closed by a stop already moved into profit; 2 were full stop losses; 1 was closed by the 4:30 PM ET end of day exit. Average winner +$387.30, average loser -$323.53. Time in a trade: median 42 minutes, shortest 17 minutes, longest about 4 hours 6 minutes. Every winner reached PT1, PT2 filled on 2 of them and PT3 was never reached; the rest of each winner was closed by the trailing stop. It traded on 8 of the 11 Tuesdays, Thursdays and Fridays from 10/1 to 10/23.

Step by step
  1. 10:15:00 AM: long 4 contracts at 7759.25, stop 7739.25.
  2. Best price during the trade: 7791.5, 32.25 pts in favor. The most it went against the entry was 0.5 pts.
  3. Once the trade was 20.25 pts in profit the trailing stop switched on and followed 10.25 pts behind the best price.

Takeaway: Part of the position took profit at a target, and the trailing stop locked in profit on the rest when price turned back.

Quick terms

ShowHide
BacktestA test of an algo's rules on past market data. These examples come from tick data backtests in the NinjaTrader Strategy Analyzer, not live trading, and results include trading fees.
Long or shortLong means buying to profit if price rises. Short means selling first to profit if price falls.
ContractOne unit of a futures market. G4M trades the Micro S&P 500 (MES).
Point and tickA point (pts) is one full unit of price movement. A tick is the smallest step price can move.
SetupThe market pattern an algo waits for before it is allowed to enter.
EntryThe moment and price where the algo opens the trade.
StopThe price where a losing trade is closed automatically. It is set before the trade is entered, so the loss is planned to be about a set size. Fees, and fills a little past the stop price, can make it a little larger.
Target (PT)A price where profit is taken. With more than one contract, part of the position exits at each target (PT1, PT2, PT3).
DEC26The December 2026 futures contract, the one these backtests traded.
Trailing stopA stop that follows a winning trade, so it locks in profit if price turns back.
Edge LeverageA setting that makes the next trade after a stop out that day use more contracts. It is off on G4M, so the contract count never changes.
CandleOne bar on the chart that covers a set amount of time, like 5 minutes. The thick body shows where price opened and closed in that time, and the thin lines above and below show the highest and lowest prices it reached.
Candle close vs touchG4M waits for a 15 minute candle to close before it enters, so its entries are on the candle close times.
Reading the chartsGreen candles closed higher than they opened, red candles closed lower. The blue arrow is the entry, a black X is an exit, the red dashed line is the stop and the green dashed lines are targets. An amber shaded box marks the setup on most charts. Times are Eastern, and each candle is labeled by the time it closed, the same as NinjaTrader.
