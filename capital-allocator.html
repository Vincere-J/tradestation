High level overview

You will: see how the tool turns 3 choices into a plan. The steps are below.

Overview
  1. Choose how much money, how much risk, and the account type.
  2. Press RUN.
  3. Read the results, then scroll down to "Your plan".
  4. Export the PDF called the Account Blueprint.
  • The plan shows how many sub accounts (separate TradeStation accounts) to open, how much goes in each, and which strategy runs in each.

  • Use a desktop or laptop. On a small screen the tool says "This tool needs a bigger screen."

Use the tool, step by step

You will: make your own plan in 9 short steps. Steps 8 and 9 are optional.

On a computer, open vincereportfolios.com/capital-allocator.

1

Pick Margin

Next to ACCOUNTS, click Margin / IRA Margin. It is already selected when the tool opens.

Use Margin even though you will not borrow. It just lets a strategy trade again the same day without waiting for money to settle.

Nothing is borrowed: the tool runs Margin at 1x, with no leverage. See Margin in Words.

2

Type your amount

In the CAPITAL "$" box, type the total you will put in across all sub accounts. Example: 30000. The tool shows it as 30,000 after you press RUN.

3

Pick a risk setting

Next to RISK, click one. Moderate is already selected.

  • Conservative: keeps at least 10% as cash. No leveraged funds, no single stocks, no futures.
  • Moderate: no futures.
  • Aggressive: adds one futures strategy, S11.
S11 is a futures strategy. If your plan lists it, your Vincere team can walk you through it before you open accounts. Which setting fits you is your choice. That is outside what we can advise on. Please consult your own tax, legal or financial advisor.

See the Strategy guide to read about each code.

4

Leave these as they are

  • Date window: All
  • Scope: All strategies
  • Rebalance between sub accounts: None (see Words)

These are the defaults.

5

Press RUN

Click the dark RUN button, right of the Risk buttons. Wait a few seconds while the tool builds your plan.

6

Read the 7 tiles

  • Starting deposit: the money you entered.
  • Total return and Net profit: the backtest gain, in percent and in dollars.
  • Max drawdown: the biggest past drop, from a high point to a low point.
  • Total trades, Annual growth, shown as CAGR (see Words), and % of winning months.

Example, $30,000 at Moderate: total return of 169.01%, a max drawdown of 9.77%, 611 trades, and 70.73% of 82 months winning.

Backtest figures, not live results. See note B

Below the tiles are more charts and tables. You do not need them to make your plan.
7

Scroll down to "Your plan"

The plan card is at the bottom, below all the charts. Scroll with the mouse over the results on the right side, not over the strategy list. The table shows each sub account, the strategy for it, and the money for it. See the Strategy guide to read about P5 and S8.

Backtest figures, not live results. See note B

8

Look at one strategy

Optional, you can skip

In the strategy list on the left, tap the i next to a strategy. A panel opens on the right with its details. Close it with the x. See the Strategy guide for what each code means.

Backtest figures, not live results. See note B

9

Pick your own strategies

Optional, you can skip

In the strategy list on the left, tick a strategy to add it, untick to remove it, or type a new amount in its box. See the Strategy guide first.

Ticking a strategy adds it at its own default amount, so your total goes up. Example: ticking P3 made $30,000 into $40,000. Press RUN to go back to the tool's plan.

The plan card follows your picks. A custom plan is your own decision. Ask your own advisor.

See 2 pictures for this step

Export your plan

You will: save your plan as a PDF called the Account Blueprint.

1

Click "Export blueprint (PDF)"

On the "Your plan" card, click Export blueprint (PDF), top right of the card.

2

A new tab opens

A new tab opens, titled Account Blueprint.

If nothing opens: the tool shows a message asking you to allow pop ups. Allow pop ups for the page, then click Export again.
3

Save it from the Print window

In the Print window that appears, set Destination to Save as PDF and click Save.

4

If the Print window closed

On the blueprint tab, click Print / Save as PDF at the top right.

What the blueprint contains

The accounts to open (for example VP 01 for P5 and VP 02 for S8), the account type, the initial deposit for each, the plan weight, and the strategy that runs in each. Keep the PDF.

Words

Sub account
A separate TradeStation account. Each one runs one strategy.
Account Blueprint
The PDF the tool makes. It lists accounts and deposits.
Backtest
How a set of rules would have done on past data. It is not a live result.
Max drawdown
The biggest past drop, from a high point to a low point, in the backtest.
Margin, in this tool
The tool says Margin only lets a sub account reuse same day sale proceeds, at 1x, with no leverage. Nothing is borrowed.
CAGR
Compound annual growth rate: the average yearly growth in the backtest.
Rebalance between sub accounts
Moving money from one sub account to another. Leave it at None.
Leveraged 3x fund
A fund built to move 3 times as much as its index, such as TQQQ.
Chosen with hindsight
Per the tool: the stock was picked after the fact, and the backtest benefits from that choice.
Futures
Contracts on a market index, such as micro Nasdaq 100.
"Coming soon to the cloud"
The tag on day trading strategies in the strategy list of the tool.
"Not available on the cloud yet"
The tag for day trading strategies in the plan view.
