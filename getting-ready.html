Step 2: Open the cash allocation tool

What you type in

  • Your account balance.
  • Cash or IRA. An IRA needs 1.5 times the cash amount behind the same size.
  • A sizing model (explained just below).

The two sizing models

  • Stable / Set Sizing is the more conservative model. Each algo trades the same fixed size, trade after trade, whatever the balance does.
  • Compounding is the more aggressive model. Each algo trades its share of the live balance, so size grows and shrinks with the account.

The choice is yours. Here's how Compounding works with four algos, each getting a 25% share:

What it shows you

  • Which algos fit and how much goes behind each one.
  • Any amount too small for another algo shows as cash left over, and that's normal. For example, a $74,906.30 account might have $70,000.00 allocated and $4,906.30 left over.

Figures in the tool are hypothetical historical simulations, not forecasts.

Tips for a smooth application

  • Use a laptop or desktop, not your phone, and turn off any VPN.
  • Google Chrome in an incognito window works best. Keep your phone nearby for the ID photos.
  • TradeStation says the online application takes about 15 minutes, and approval typically comes within 24 hours.
  • For your ID: clear and sharp, no glare, all four corners showing, with the desk or table visible behind it.
  • For your selfie: plain background, no hat and no glasses. If a photo gets rejected, just retake it in better light.

Funding

  • Skip the funding prompt during the application. Once TradeStation approves you, it's fine to fund the account.
  • Let me know once you're funded, so I can keep track for you.

IRA clients: one extra step
